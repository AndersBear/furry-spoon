The Gibbs Free Energy equation is:

ΔG = ΔH - TΔS

The equation balances total energy change and disorder at a specific temperature.

ΔG: change in free energy
ΔH: change in heat
T: temperature
ΔS: change in entropy (randomness, disorder)
